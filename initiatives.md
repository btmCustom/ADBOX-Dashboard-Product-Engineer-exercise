# Harbor Point Bank: Q3 2026 Marketing Initiatives

The client runs three initiatives in this period. Each is live on some combination of Meta, Google Ads and Amazon DSP.
Campaign names in the platform exports were created by different teams and do not follow one convention.

1. **Fall Rate Promo**
   Promotional CD and savings rate campaign. Runs on all three platforms.

2. **Checking Account Launch**
   Launch of the new no-fee checking product. Runs on all three platforms.

3. **Home Equity Spring**
   Home equity line of credit campaign. The initiative was named in the spring planning cycle and kept its name; it is live in this period.

Not every campaign in the exports belongs to an initiative. Some are always-on, internal, or test campaigns.
